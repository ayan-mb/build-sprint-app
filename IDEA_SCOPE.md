The idea, in one line
The tool is to help brands to find their mentions in AI search tools like Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and the sources (websites) where the mentions have been picked up from and writes content that can help the brand to improve their mentions.

The core loop (user stories, written by me):
When buyers use AI to research financial products or services, marketing teams need to know if they are being recommended and why. The tool will help (1) Identify which prompts are leading to their mentions (2)The competitors who are getting mentioned (3) Which sources are leading to the mentions. (4) Draft the content
The tool will provide the answer to if a buyer is searching for a personal loan for specific CIBIL scores, who is being recommended and do the platforms give option to redirect to their website or their journeys to apply for the loan
